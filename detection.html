Methods

How well can we detect evaluation awareness?

33,369 prompts · 7 held-out test sources · 3 models

A detector here gives each prompt a score for how much the model treats it as an evaluation rather than real use. Every white-box detector is trained and tuned on the same dataset of 33,369 eval and deployment prompts, then tested on 16,261 prompts from 7 sources held out of training. The scores measure how eval-like the model’s internal state looks, not a verified belief.

  • J++ is the best detector where a released J++ lens exists. On Qwen3.6-27B its trained readout reaches test AUROC 0.768, ahead of the logit lens (0.707) and the linear probe (0.706). No detector beats a TF-IDF classifier on the prompt words (0.866).
  • Elsewhere, the linear probe is the default. On Qwen3-8B and Gemma-4-12B-it the best lens differs by model (R-lens 0.642 vs probe 0.613; released J-lens 0.680 vs 0.621), and our own J++ fits score only 0.625 and 0.584. The probe has the best macro AUROC on Qwen3-8B (0.747) and is within 0.01 of the best on Gemma.
  • The oracle lens is worse. Its readouts mostly describe the format of the answer the model is about to write, not its situation. For a benchmark question (eval): “Answer: C Explanation: This is a multiple choice question from SAT Subject TestScience”; for a real user question rewritten as multiple choice (deployment): “Answer: The correct chronological order is B. Explanation: This question tests knowledge”. The LLM judge rates these both 8 out of 10. Test AUROC: judge 0.536, keywords 0.424, bullet LR 0.640.
  • The A.21 score as published is weak. The fixed eval-word contrast gets test AUROC 0.330–0.657 over all 12 model × lens pairs (0.330–0.405, below chance, on Gemma). On the two smaller models it is below 0.5 on validation for 7 of 8 lenses and flags up to 100% of real WildChat prompts.
  • Choosing a variant on validation data is unreliable. The variant with the best validation AUROC was also best on the held-out test sources in 5 of 12 model × lens pairs. On Qwen3.6-27B those picks score 0.918–0.940 on validation and 0.453–0.587 on test.

Methods

MethodReadsMeasures
Linear probeResidual stream at one layerA direction trained to separate eval from deployment prompts
J++ / J-lens / R-lensResidual stream decoded into words by a workspace lensHow strongly eval-related words are decoded; the main score (lens-lr) is a classifier over 190 such words
Logit lensResidual stream decoded by the model’s own output layerSame word readout, no extra lens
Oracle lensShort text bullets a trained lens writes about layer 42Whether the bullets describe a test: scored by an LLM judge, eval-word counts, or a word classifier
TF-IDF baselinePrompt words only, no modelHow much of the label is visible in surface text
Black-box methodsThe model’s reasoning (CoT monitor), its answers to follow-up questions, its yes/no token probabilities, or an outside judge reading the transcriptVerbalized or self-reported awareness; used on agent transcripts on the other pages, not scored here
