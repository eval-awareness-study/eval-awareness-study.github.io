How to read this page
  • Design. 17 beliefs about being evaluated were planted into each model by several text methods and by steering, on 11 benchmarks (the first 50 tasks of each, fewer where a benchmark is smaller; 2 samples per task). Each belief is compared with the same method's neutral text (its matched control).
  • Difference: rate with the belief minus rate with the control, in percentage points (points), paired by task; 95% interval from resampling tasks.
  • Labels (the dashboard's rule, no multiple-testing correction): Significant = two-sided p < .05 (sign-flip test over tasks) with more than two tasks; Not significant; Inconclusive = two tasks or fewer; Unavailable = no p. With hundreds of cells, many Significant labels are expected by chance alone.
  • Taken on: whether the model took the belief on (its answer to a readout question before the reply, or a question after the run, moved toward the belief; for planted thoughts, at least half the coded continuations refer to the thought). Halves: the two sample halves are independent draws on the same tasks; 'agree' means both point the same way as the whole. These are shown as facts, not as a correction.
  • Belief groups were defined after seeing gpt-oss-120b's first results (post hoc).
